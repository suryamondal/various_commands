\begin{keypoint}
\textbf{Status.} A three-machine pool is running. Remote submission works from a
fourth machine with no login, no shared filesystem and no home directory on the
entry node. All three policy behaviours --- suspension, eviction and admission
closure --- have been observed firing under real load, not merely configured.
The entry node survives a reboot with its queue, configuration and policy intact.
\end{keypoint}
